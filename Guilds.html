    Guilds

    Guilds reward you for completing skill-based quests. Each guild has 10 levels. Advancing to the next level requires two things at your current level: completing that level's quest below, and completing enough of that guild's daily quests.

    Dailies Required Per Level

    Guild Level Dailies Required
    0 2
    1 3
    2 4
    3 5
    4 7
    5 9
    6 12
    7 15
    8 20
    9 25

    Guild Hall Bonus

    Upgrading the Guild Hall building reduces quest requirements. See the Buildings page for upgrade costs.

    Guild Hall Tier Quest Requirement
    0 No reduction
    0 -10% guild quest requirements
    1 -20% guild quest requirements
    2 -30% guild quest requirements

    Daily Guild Quests

    Each guild also offers up to four rotating daily quests, refreshed at 6 AM, that count toward the dailies requirement for your current guild level.
