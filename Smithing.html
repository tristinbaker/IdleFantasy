    Smithing

    Smelt ores into bars, then forge equipment. All smithing is done in sessions.

    Hammers

    Equip a hammer to multiply how many items you can smith per session. Higher-tier hammers require a higher Smithing level.

    Tool Level Required Efficiency
    Heirloom Hammer 1 4.50×
    Bronze Hammer 1 1.00×
    Iron Hammer 15 1.25×
    Steel Hammer 30 1.50×
    Mithril Hammer 55 1.75×
    Dwarven Hammer 60 2.50×
    Adamantite Hammer 70 2.00×
    Runite Hammer 85 2.25×
    Divine Hammer 85 3.00×

    Hammer tier efficiency multiplier

    Depending on the difference in tier between the tool used for smithing and the item you are smithing, you can receive an additional mult to your standard tool efficiency. The effect of this is that you can craft items with lower tiers potentially much faster than items with higher tiers if you have higher-tiered tools.

    For normal tools, their tier is determined by their minimum level required to equip. For the Heirloom Hammer, its tier is defined by your current smithing level. The heirloom's tool efficiency itself is calculated separately as described in Heirlooms.

    For items, their tier depends on the minimum required level for smithing.

    The level ranges for each tier and the associated tools are defined as below:

    Tier Level Range Tools
    1 1-14 Bronze Hammer
    2 15-29 Iron Hammer
    3 30-54 Steel Hammer
    4 55-69 Mithril Hammer, Dwarven Hammer
    5 70-84 Adamantite Hammer
    6 ≥85 Runite Hammer, Divine Hammer

    The exact multiplier you get applied to your tool efficiency depends on the tier difference as calculated using the formula: Tier difference=tool tier−item tierTier\ difference = tool\ tier - item\ tierTier difference=tool tier−item tier.
