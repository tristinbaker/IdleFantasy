    Firemaking

    Burn logs from your inventory to gain Firemaking XP. Each log burned produces ashes which can be scattered for bonus Prayer XP and can also be used for bonuses in farming, herblore, and runecrafting.

    Log Level Required XP / Log Burned
    Logs 1 40
    Oak Logs 15 60
    Willow Logs 30 90
    Maple Logs 45 135
    Yew Logs 60 203
    Magic Logs 75 303
    Redwood Logs 90 455

    Tinderboxes

    Equip a tinderbox to multiply how many logs you can burn per session. Higher-tier tinderboxes require a higher Firemaking level.

    Tool Level Required Efficiency
    Heirloom Tinderbox 1 4.50×
    Bronze Tinderbox 1 1.00×
    Iron Tinderbox 15 1.25×
    Steel Tinderbox 30 1.50×
    Mithril Tinderbox 55 1.75×
    Dwarven Tinderbox 60 2.50×
    Adamantite Tinderbox 70 2.00×
    Runite Tinderbox 85 2.25×
    Divine Tinderbox 85 3.00×

    Tinderbox tier efficiency multiplier

    Depending on the difference in tier between the tool used for burning and the item you are burning, you can receive an additional mult to your standard tool efficiency. The effect of this is that you can craft items with lower tiers potentially much faster than items with higher tiers if you have higher-tiered tools.

    For normal tools, their tier is determined by their minimum level required to equip. For the Heirloom Tinderbox, its tier is defined by your current firemaking level. The heirloom's tool efficiency itself is calculated separately as described in Heirlooms.

    For items, their tier depends on the minimum required level for burning.

    The level ranges for each tier and the associated tools are defined as below:

    Tier Level Range Tools
    1 1-14 Bronze Tinderbox
    2 15-29 Iron Tinderbox
    3 30-54 Steel Tinderbox
    4 55-69 Mithril Tinderbox, Dwarven Tinderbox
    5 70-84 Adamantite Tinderbox
    6 ≥85 Runite Tinderbox, Divine Tinderbox
