    Mining

    Mine ores from the earth. Gems have a small chance to drop alongside any ore.

    Ore Level Required XP / Ore Description
    Rune Essence 1 15 Raw magical stone used to craft all types of runes.
    Copper Ore 1 18 Common ore. Combine with tin ore to smelt bronze bars.
    Tin Ore 1 18 Common ore. Combine with copper ore to smelt bronze bars.
    Stone 10 12 A block of stone. Used for construction.
    Iron Ore 15 35 Versatile ore used to smelt iron and steel bars.
    Silver Ore 20 40 Precious ore used to craft silver bars and jewellery.
    Coal 30 50 Used as a fuel component when smelting steel and higher-tier bars.
    Gold Ore 40 65 Precious ore used to craft gold bars and jewellery.
    Mithril Ore 55 80 A rare ore with magical properties.
    Adamantite Ore 70 95 Extremely hard and very rare ore.
    Platinum Ore 80 100 A rare platinum ore used for platinum bars.
    Runite Ore 85 125 The rarest mineable ore, used for the strongest equipment.

    Gems

    Each time you mine an ore (with higher pickaxe efficiencies rolling more often), you have a chance to receive each of the associated gems in the table below:

    Gems Drop Rate Description
    Sapphire 2% A blue gemstone used in jewellery crafting.
    Emerald 1% A green gemstone of moderate rarity.
    Ruby 0.5% A red gemstone. Rare and valuable.
    Diamond 0.2% The rarest and most valuable gemstone.

    Pickaxe tier efficiency multiplier

    Depending on the difference in tier between the pickaxe used for mining, and the level required for mining ores, you can receive an additional mult to your standard tool efficiency. The effect of this is that when you're mining lower tier ores your tool has a much higher efficiency with all the bonuses that entails.

    For normal tools, their tier is determined by their minimum level required to equip. For the Heirloom Pickaxe, its tier is defined by your current mining level. The heirloom's tool efficiency itself is calculated separately as described in Heirlooms.

    For ores, the tier depends on the minimum required level for mining the relevant ores.

    The level ranges for each tier are shown below:

    Tier Level Range Tools Ores
    1 1-14 Bronze Pickaxe Rune Essence, Copper Ore, Tin Ore, Stone
    2 15-29 Iron Pickaxe Iron Ore, Silver Ore
    3 30-54 Steel Pickaxe, Deep Iron Pickaxe Coal, Gold Ore
    4 55-69 Mithril Pickaxe, Dwarven Pickaxe Mithril Ore
    5 70-84 Adamantite Pickaxe, Forge Pickaxe Adamantite Ore, Platinum Ore
    6 ≥85 Runite Pickaxe, Divine Pickaxe Runite Ore
