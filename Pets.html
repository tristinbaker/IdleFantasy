    Pets

    Pets are rare rewards found throughout the game: boss drops, skill session loot, and prizes from special content like the Carnival shop. Each pet provides a permanent passive bonus once collected.

    Pet Source Bonus Description
    🦑 Baby Kraken Kraken raid (1/100 per kill) +5% Cooking XP A tiny kraken that improves your cooking abilities.
    🪙 Coin Drake Mercantile (6% chance per session) +10% Mercantile XP A dragon hatchling raised on a diet of gold coins. It has an uncanny nose for profit.
    🐉 Dragon Whelp King Black Dragon raid (1/100 per kill) +5% Combat XP A young dragon that boosts your combat prowess.
    🦋 Ember Moth Firemaking (6% chance per session) +10% Firemaking XP Drawn to flame for so long it became one. Its wings shimmer with residual heat.
    🌳 Ent Woodcutting (6% chance per session) +10% Woodcutting XP An ancient tree spirit that aids your woodcutting.
    🪶 Feather Drake Fletching (6% chance per session) +10% Fletching XP A miniature dragon made entirely of arrow fletching. Surprisingly aerodynamic.
    ⚒️ Forge Imp Smithing (6% chance per session) +10% Smithing XP A mischievous imp that lives inside the forge. Its heat-hardened hide is impervious to burns.
    💎 Gem Golem Crafting (6% chance per session) +10% Crafting XP A tiny creature sculpted from gem shards and polishing dust. Keeps your tools sharp.
    🪿 Golden Goose Grand Monument stage 4 (Gilded) +10% Coins A regal goose that roosts atop the gilded Monument. Coins simply seem to find its keeper.
    🔥 Gothic Emberling Gothic Ember raid (1/200 per kill) +5% Magic XP A tiny demon of fire and shadow that enhances your magic.
    🧚 Graceling Sprite Agility (6% chance per session) +10% Agility XP A nimble fairy born from thousands of laps run in silence. Guides your feet with uncanny grace.
    🌱 Grave Sprout Gloomharvest event reward (125 tokens) +5% Farming XP A stubborn little seedling that took root in a barrow mound. It knows a thing or two about growing.
    🌿 Herb Wisp Herblore (6% chance per session) +10% Herblore XP A spirit born from the vapour of a thousand brewed potions. Its presence sharpens the mind.
    😈 Imp Pet Demon Lord raid (1/100 per kill) +5% Prayer XP A mischievous imp that enhances your prayer training.
    🤹 Juggling Imp Carnival prize shop (4,000 tickets) +3% All XP A mischievous imp that juggles your worries away. Boosts all skill XP by 3%.
    🎃 Lantern Wisp The Hollow King raid (Gloomharvest event) +5% Combat XP A grinning gourd-light that escaped the Hollow King's crown and now lights your way to battle.
    🗿 Rock Golem Mining (6% chance per session) +10% Mining XP A living rock formation that enhances your mining prowess.
    🔮 Rune Sprite Runecrafting (6% chance per session) +10% Runecrafting XP A crystallised fragment of magical energy that escaped the Runecrafting altar.
    🦊 Shadow Fox Thieving (6% chance per session) +10% Thieving XP A cunning fox that materialised from the shadows of a thousand pickpocket attempts.
    🔥 Solstice Sprite Sol Invictus raid (1/100 per kill during the Summer Solstice event) +5% Combat XP A tiny ember spirit from the Sunspire Solstice event that boosts your combat prowess.
    🗿 Stone Guardian Infinite Tower milestone: Floor 100 +5% Combat XP An ancient stone golem that guards the tower's secrets. Boosts combat XP.
    🌿 Tangleroot Farming harvest (1/1000 per harvest) +10% Farming XP A magical plant creature that enhances your farming.
    🪵 Timber Sprite Construction (6% chance per session) +10% Construction XP A wood spirit awakened from sawdust and lumber. It knows exactly where every nail should go.
    🐟 Tiny Templar Fishing (6% chance per session) +10% Fishing XP A small fish that boosts your fishing expertise.
    💫 Void Wisp Void Sovereign raid (1/333 per kill) +5% Combat XP A fragment of the Void Sovereign's essence, bound to those who bested its master. Amplifies combat experience.
