    Axes

    Axe tier efficiency multiplier

    Depending on the difference in tier between the axe used for chopping, and the level required for chopping trees, you can receive an additional mult to your standard tool efficiency. The effect of this is that when you're chopping lower tier trees your tool has a much higher efficiency with all the bonuses that entails.

    For normal tools, their tier is determined by their minimum level required to equip. For the Heirloom Axe, its tier is defined by your current woodcutting level. The heirloom's tool efficiency itself is calculated separately as described in Heirlooms.

    For trees, the tier depends on the minimum required level for chopping the relevant trees.

    The level ranges for each tier are shown below:

    Tier Level Range Tools Trees
    1 1-14 Bronze Axe Tree
    2 15-29 Iron Axe Oak Tree
    3 30-54 Steel Axe, Ironwood Axe, Mithril Axe Willow Tree, Maple Tree
    4 55-69 Adamantite Axe, Dwarven Axe Yew Tree
    5 70-84 Sanctum Axe, Runite Axe Magic Tree
    6 ≥85 Divine Axe Redwood Tree
