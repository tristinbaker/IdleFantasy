    Fishing

    Catch fish and aquatic treasures from the waters.

    Fishing drops

    When fishing, for each frame (of which there are 60 frames per session), the drops you receive depend on a random roll. 80% of the time, you will receive the fish that you initially selected with the quantity multiplied by the tool efficiency. 20% of the time, you'll receive random drops from the rare items table.

    XP is always calculated as selected fish XP×tool efficiency×pet boostsselected\ fish\ XP \times tool\ efficiency \times pet\ boostsselected fish XP×tool efficiency×pet boosts independent of drops.

    Fishing rod tier efficiency multiplier

    Depending on the difference in tier between the fishing rod used for fishing, and the level required for fishing fish, you can receive an additional mult to your standard tool efficiency. The effect of this is that when you're fishing lower tier fish your tool has a much higher efficiency with all the bonuses that entails.

    For normal tools, their tier is determined by their minimum level required to equip. For the Heirloom Fishing Rod, its tier is defined by your current fishing level. The heirloom's tool efficiency itself is calculated separately as described in Heirlooms.

    For fish, the tier depends on the minimum required level for fishing the relevant fish.

    The level ranges for each tier are shown below:

    Tier Level Range Tools Fish
    1 1-14 Bronze Fishing Rod Raw Shrimp, Raw Sardine, Raw Herring
