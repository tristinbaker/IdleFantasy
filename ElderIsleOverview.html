    Elder Isle

    Elder Isle is Idle Fantasy's second region. It sits behind the mainland's final skill unlock, is opened by beating a boss that requires the rest of the game to be substantially built out, and adds its own skill tracks, dungeons, story chain, and best-in-slot armor set to chase once the mainland has stopped feeding you goals.

    The isle is a location swap, not a menu. When you sail, every bottom tab (Home, Skills, Combat, Quests, Profile) switches to its isle variant. You return to the mainland from the same button that took you across.

    Unlocking the isle

    1. Reach Construction 90 and build the Dock in your town.
    2. The Dock offers the Voyage quest, which culminates in the Sea Serpent raid boss. Bring an endgame combat kit.
    3. Defeat the Sea Serpent once. The isle is now permanently unlocked, and a Set sail for Elder Isle button appears on the mainland Home tab.

    The first time you land, a splash explains what changes. It shows once per character.

    What changes when you sail

    • Skills tab shows only the Elder skills that exist on the isle. Sessions run against isle activities, use elder gear, and pay into a separate XP pool.
    • Combat tab shows only isle dungeons and the Last Elder boss. Isle sessions never touch mainland enemies.
    • Quests tab becomes the isle main story chain (see Story & Lore). The mainland quest tab is out of reach while sailed.
    • Home tab shows the isle currency chips (Ancient Sigils, Elder Essence, Elder Bones), an armor set tracker, and buttons for the Elder Isle Shop, Elder Armor Master, and the Lore Master.
    • Profile > Skills gains a Mainland / Elder toggle. This is the one place both track sets are visible at once, whichever land you are currently in.

    The bonus flow rule

    • Mainland bonuses do not apply to the isle. No pet, cape, church blessing, prestige node, XP boost, or heirloom mirror touches an isle session. Elder XP scales only with your elder skill level and any sigils you have embedded.
    • Isle sigil bonuses apply everywhere. sigil stones you embed into Elder gear buff XP, coin, loot, and essence on both isle sessions and mainland sessions. Get the bonus once, keep it forever.
    • Race, ironman status, and the shared inventory carry across. You do not have separate inventories or currencies for the isle. Coins are shared.

    What to chase

    The isle's headline goal is the 8-piece Elder set (Elder armor set), which is best-in-slot armor for both regions. Every piece consumes 5 Ancient Sigils to craft. Sigils drop from the isle dungeon rare-drop tables (see Isle Dungeons & Bosses). Neither raid boss drops them.

    Alongside the set, sigil stones slot into your Elder gear via the Sigil Embedder for permanent stat bonuses.

    Once you have the full set, the last quest in the chain, the fight against the Last Elder, unlocks. Winning it grants the Ancient Signet accessory and the Isle Champion title.
