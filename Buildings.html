    Buildings

    The Builder's Workshop in the Town screen lets you upgrade the permanent town buildings. Each building provides a passive bonus that scales with its tier. Upgrades require coins and Construction materials crafted at the Construction skill.

    Buildings start at Tier 0 (no bonus) and unlock higher tiers at higher Construction levels. Every Construction level also reduces upgrade coin and material costs by 0.5%, up to 49.5% at level 99.

    Inn

    Increases the XP gained by both workers each session.

    Tier Construction Level Coin Cost Materials Bonuses
    0 — — — No bonus
    1 20 50,000 200× Plank, 100× Oak Plank, 500× Iron Nail +10% Worker XP per session
    2 45 250,000 500× Oak Plank, 200× Willow Plank, 1,500× Steel Nail +20% Worker XP per session
    3 70 1,000,000 1,000× Willow Plank, 1,000× Maple Plank, 3,000× Mithril Nail +30% Worker XP per session

    3 75 1,500,000 800× Maple Plank, 400× Yew Plank, 2,500× Mithril Nail Cooldowns reduced to 5 minutes, +15% idle ticket chance
