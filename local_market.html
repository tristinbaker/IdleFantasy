    Local Market

    Minimum level Coin cost
    1 2,000

    Trade common goods at the local market. Returns are modest but variable.

    XP ranges

    The XP you receive doing the Local Market trading route depends on your mercantile level as shown in the table below.

    Mercantile Level XP range Average xp
    1-10 900-1,500 1,200
    10-20 1,200-2,100 1,650
    >20 1,500-2,400 1,950

    Coin returns

    Like XP, the coins you receive depends on your mercantile level and are shown in the ranges below

    Mercantile Level Coin range Average coins
    1-10 600-3,960 2,280
    10-20 780-4,380 2,580
    >20 840-4,680 2,760
    All data is auto-generated from game files and stays up to date with the latest release. · Report an issue · GitHub
