    Main Story

    Idle Fantasy has an optional main story. You play a Duster, a contractor sent by the Triptych to settle an uncharted island, guided over a sending stone by Thaddius Cantor. The story unfolds through short notes as you clear the twelve story dungeons, from the Farm to the Ancient Temple, and ends with a fight against Kur’zul.

    The story never locks anything. Every dungeon, skill and item works the same whether you play it, skip it, or stop halfway.

    Starting the story

    • New characters are asked once, right after character setup, whether to begin the story now. "Not now" leaves it for later.
    • Anyone else can start it any time from the Story tab on the Quests screen with Begin the story.

    How notes work

    • Notes pop up over whatever screen you're on: when you start a story dungeon, when you finish an objective, and when you finish a chapter. A note waits until the session summary on Home is closed.
    • Some notes have several pages; use Next to read on. Read later keeps a note unread in the Story tab.
    • Every note you've received can be reread from the Story tab, grouped by chapter.
    • Early on, Thaddius suggests naming the island. A box to name it appears once after his welcome note, and you can rename it from the Story tab.

    Two settings control the story:

    • Story pop-ups: turn off to skip the pop-ups. New notes then wait unread in the Story tab.
    • Story tab in Quests: turn off to hide the Story tab and its unread count entirely.

    Chapters

    Chapter Objective Reward
    Prologue: The Expedition Corps None, it plays as soon as you begin None
    1: Landfall Complete a mining, fishing or woodcutting session, cook shrimp, smelt a bronze bar, and clear the Farm without dying 1,000 coins and the Duster title
    2: Imp Cavern Clear Imp Cavern without dying 1,000 coins
    3: Goblin Cave Clear Goblin Cave without dying 2,000 coins
    4: Spider Den Clear Spider Den without dying 4,000 coins
    5: Bandit Camp Clear Bandit Camp without dying 8,000 coins and the Obsidian Key
    6: Undead Crypt Clear Undead Crypt without dying 16,000 coins
    7: Fortress Ruins Clear Fortress Ruins without dying 32,000 coins
    8: Orc Stronghold Clear Orc Stronghold without dying 50,000 coins
    9: Volcanic Depths Clear Volcanic Depths without dying 75,000 coins and the Essence of the Mountain
    10: Dragon's Lair Clear Dragon's Lair without dying 100,000 coins and the Dragon’s Molt
    11: Infernal Stronghold Clear Infernal Stronghold without dying while holding the Essence of the Mountain and Dragon’s Molt 150,000 coins and the Eye of Chaos
    12: Ancient Temple Clear Ancient Temple without dying while holding the Obsidian Key and Eye of Chaos 200,000 coins, and unlocks Kur’zul
    13: Kur’zul Defeat Kur’zul The Bane of Kur’zul title
    • Objectives only count while their chapter is active. Clears from before the chapter started don't count, so the story plays out one dungeon at a time even if you've already beaten everything.
    • Dungeon clears count only if you don't die. Sessions run by town workers and anything on the Elder Isle never count.
    • Chapters complete on their own. There is no claim step; the reward and the next chapter arrive with the chapter's final note.

    Keepsakes

    Four story items are picked up along the way:

    Keepsake How you get it Used for
    Obsidian Key Finishing the Bandit Camp chapter Needed, with the Eye of Chaos, for the Ancient Temple chapter
    Essence of the Mountain Finishing the Volcanic Depths chapter Used up in the Infernal Stronghold chapter
    Dragon’s Molt Finishing the Dragon's Lair chapter Used up in the Infernal Stronghold chapter
    Eye of Chaos Finishing the Infernal Stronghold chapter Needed, with the Obsidian Key, for the Ancient Temple chapter

    Keepsakes can't be sold, and Sell Junk always skips them. If one goes missing while a chapter still needs it, clearing the dungeon that first gave it to you hands it back.

    The finale

    Finishing the Ancient Temple chapter unlocks Kur’zul, a boss that only appears for players whose story has reached him. Defeating him plays the ending and finishes the story. He stays available afterwards, and he never counts toward the Godslayer title.

    Titles

    • Duster: finish Landfall.
    • Bane of Kur’zul: defeat Kur'zul.

    Both are listed with every other title on the Titles page.
